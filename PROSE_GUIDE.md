# Prose guide — MSSP paper writing rules

How the paper should be written and edited. (A copy of this also lives in Claude's
memory folder as `feedback_prose_guide.md`, which is auto-loaded each session; keep
the two in sync if you change one.)

## 1. Concise, plain sentences
- One claim per sentence; short and direct over long multi-clause sentences.
- Avoid colon-splices and stacked subordinate clauses (a colon joining two independent clauses is the flagged pattern).
- Cross-reference a section rather than re-explaining mechanics inline.
- **Bad example:** the original Diebold–Mariano "unit of analysis" sentence — three ideas welded via a colon + "rather than" contrast + "so that" sub-clause.

## 2. Integrate figure/table references grammatically
- Prefer "As Table~\ref{} shows, …" / "Figure~\ref{} shows …" over a trailing "(Table~\ref{})".
- **Exception:** keep a bare parenthetical only where forcing it into the sentence would bloat a claim that is really about something else.

## 3. Hedge claims; scope to what a single-setup study supports
- Soften definitive words: **"first"** → "to our knowledge, the first" (or verify it is genuinely first); avoid "conclusive / proves".
- Use "indicates" instead of "proves", "demonstrates", and "shows" to hedge more.

## 4. Vary stiff connectives
- Soften or drop som of "Furthermore / In addition / Therefore / Consequently" — replace with "also" / "so" or nothing. Vary them; don't mechanically swap every one to the same word or remove them. They serve purposes some places.
