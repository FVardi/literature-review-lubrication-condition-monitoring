
\section{Literature Selection}
\label{sec:search}

\subsection{Search Approach}
This is a narrative review witha  structured search. Relevant literature was identified through keyword searches in Scopus, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar and ResearchGate, carried out mainly between October and December 2025, with later additions up to November 2026. Searches used combinations of terms from the following keyword groups:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Application domain:} lubrication, lubrication condition monitoring, film thickness, lubrication degradation, lubricant starvation, grease condition, oil condition.
  \item \textbf{Component:} ball bearing, rolling element bearing, roller bearing.
  \item \textbf{Sensing modality:} vibration, acoustic emission, ultrasound, sound, microphone, thermal, infrared, electrical, capacitance, impedance, optical, magnetic, inductive.
  \item \textbf{Signal processing:} signal processing, feature extraction, time domain, frequency domain, wavelet, machine learning, deep learning, neural network, regression, classification.
  \item \textbf{Monitoring mode:} online, in-line, in-situ, condition monitoring.
\end{itemize}

The searches were supplemented by citation-graph exploration (Connected Papers) and technical documentation from bearing manufacturers (SKF, Schaeffler).

\subsection{Selection Criteria}
